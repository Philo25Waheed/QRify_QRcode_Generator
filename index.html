About The Platform

Why QRify?

QRify is a lightweight browser-based QR Code Generator designed to make creating and customizing QR codes simple, fast, and private.

Traditional online QR generators frequently monetize by redirecting your links through centralized tracking servers, slapping arbitrary limits on high-res downloads, or bombarding users with advertising. QRify takes the opposite approach: 100% client-side computation, open web standards, complete privacy, and vector SVG exports for print perfection.

Privacy Policy & Architecture

How QRify protects your confidential information

1. Zero Data Transmission: Every single QR code generated in QRify is rendered entirely within your browser's JavaScript engine. No payload, Wi-Fi password, or URL is ever transmitted across the network.

2. Local History Only: When enabled, your history is stored solely inside your browser's isolated localStorage database on your local machine. It is never synced to any remote database.

3. Works Completely Offline: You can disconnect your internet connection or save this page locally, and QRify will continue to function flawlessly.
